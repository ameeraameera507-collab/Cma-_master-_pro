Part 1

Financial Planning, Performance & Analytics

Part 1 covers how a management accountant plans, measures, and reports on the financial performance of a business — from external reporting rules through to modern data analytics.

TopicWhat it coversWeight
External Financial Reporting Decisions Financial statements, recognition and measurement of assets and liabilities, and the standards behind them. 15%
Planning, Budgeting & Forecasting Building operating budgets, forecasting techniques, and analyzing budget-to-actual variances. 20%
Performance Management Responsibility centers, variance analysis, balanced scorecards, and performance measures like ROI and residual income. 20%
Cost Management Costing systems, cost behavior, activity-based costing, and cost-volume-profit analysis. 15%
Internal Controls Control frameworks such as COSO, risk of fraud and error, and the design of a sound control environment. 15%
Technology & Analytics Data governance, descriptive vs. predictive analytics, and how technology supports decision-making. 15%
Part 2

Strategic Financial Management

Part 2 moves from internal operations to strategic decisions — reading financial statements, raising capital, evaluating investments, managing risk, and acting ethically while doing it.

TopicWhat it coversWeight
Financial Statement Analysis Ratio analysis, liquidity, profitability, and reading between the lines of a company's reported numbers. 20%
Corporate Finance Sources of capital, cost of capital, capital structure, and how firms raise and manage money. 20%
Decision Analysis Payback period, net present value, relevant costs, and other tools for evaluating business decisions. 25%
Risk Management Identifying, measuring, and hedging enterprise, market, and operational risks. 10%
Investment Decisions Capital budgeting, portfolio risk and return, and valuation of securities. 15%
Professional Ethics The IMA Statement of Ethical Professional Practice and resolving ethical conflicts at work. 10%
